Ippomed native DOM renderer

This small public folder is selected with EXPO_PUBLIC_FOLDER=public-native for
native builds. Do not copy the anatomy GLBs here: public geometry is downloaded
on demand from the pinned HTTPS anatomy asset host.

BodyParts3D source notices, license links and per-file provenance are published
alongside those models at /anatomy/bodyparts3d/ and
/anatomy/bodyparts3d-complete/ on the application's public host.

The renderer and its manifests are bundled with the application, together with
the Three.js and Meshoptimizer code used to render the original meshes.
