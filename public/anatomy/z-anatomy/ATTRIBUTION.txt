Excluded physical objects: 206 for licence/provenance review, 59 technical exclusions. Full decisions and source evidence are recorded in manifest.json. Source definitions, Wikipedia text, translations and upstream application code were not copied or executed.



Adaptation: evaluated source world coordinates converted once from Blender Z-up metres to Y-up metres, bilateral object transforms retained, reflected winding corrected. HIGH retains every evaluated source triangle. Every valid nonzero authored corner normal is retained before explicitly bounded quantization and lossless compression/reordering; the exactly zero source-normal exception is documented below. Preview alone is simplified with a 0.5 mm estimated appearance error limit. Missing anatomy, trajectories, fibres and radii are never generated.


Authored normal exception: 230 exactly zero source corner normals in 10 objects were undefined in the source. Only these lighting normals were derived from their incident source triangle faces. Every nonzero authored normal, source position and source triangle remains unchanged before bounded encoding. Source normal-array SHA-256 and repaired corner indices are recorded per object in manifest.json.
